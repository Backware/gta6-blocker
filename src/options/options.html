GTA 6 Blocker settings

Keywords

One per line, not case sensitive. Spaces also match dashes, dots and no space at all, so GTA 6 catches "GTA-6" and "gta6". Hashtags match by prefix, so GTA also catches #gtaonline.

Allowed sites

One domain per line (for example example.com). Nothing is blocked on these sites or their subdomains.
